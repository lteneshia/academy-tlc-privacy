Privacy Policy

App Name: Academy T.L.C. Bible Study Journal
Effective Date: November 23, 2026
Contact Email: academytlc.ministry@gmail.com

1. Local-First Personal Journal Data

Your personal Bible study notes, prayer logs, SOAP reflections, and reading progress are stored locally on your own device. We do not collect, sell, or read your private journal entries.

2. Optional Community Prayer Wall

If you voluntarily submit a prayer request to the Community Prayer Wall, the name (or "Anonymous"), title, and prayer details you enter are stored securely in our cloud database (Supabase) for admin review before appearing on the public Prayer Wall. A random device token is stored locally on your device so you may mark your own prayer as answered or remove it at any time.

3. No Advertising or Third-Party Tracking

Academy T.L.C. Bible Study Journal contains no third-party advertising networks and does not sell or rent user data to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for general audiences and adults and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Contact & Data Deletion

To request removal of a submitted Community Prayer Wall entry or ask questions about this policy, contact us at academytlc.ministry@gmail.com.
